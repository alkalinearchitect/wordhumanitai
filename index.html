The 4th Space

We don't only forecast the cold.
We build the warm room.

The 4th Space is a physical, social venue — sauna, cold plunge, contrast therapy — that combats loneliness by bringing people together. It is the delivery layer that turns our predictions into human presence. Where the model says pressure is breaking, the 4th Space is the front door our teams already trust.

Mental healthFrom crisis to company
Our forecast flags the places carrying the heaviest anxiety — Spelthorne, Adur, Glasgow sit highest in the live data. The 4th Space meets that weight before it becomes a crisis: a warm, non-clinical room where asking for nothing is allowed. It helps the area think: “I am not a case file — I am someone who got warm with other people today.”
AgeingFrom isolation to ritual
Older people are the quietest pressure in the data and the loneliest in the room. A regular contrast-therapy circle gives the week a shape — a reason to leave the flat, a face that expects you. It helps the area think: “I still belong to something that happens on a schedule.”
PovertyFrom scarcity to dignity
When money is thin, public warmth is a luxury. A subsidised 4th Space restores it as a right. It sits beside debt advice and cost-of-living support, but it asks for nothing in return. It helps the area think: “I am cared for, not managed.”
HomelessnessFrom the street to steadiness
Under the 2026 Rough Sleeping Strategy, prevention is the brief. The 4th Space is the steady, repeated contact that keeps someone off the pavement — a place that is theirs, not a waiting room. It helps the area think: “There is a warm address I can name.”
NHS strainFrom queue to community
Every avoided lonely evening is a call to the GP that never had to happen. The 4th Space is neighbourhood health delivered by presence, not a prescription. It helps the area think: “My wellbeing is held by people, not just a service.”
