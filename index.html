02The 4th Space

A sauna and cold-plunge venue where lonely people actually show up — and stop being lonely.

Warm in.
Cold out.
Less alone.
Contrast therapy · connection by design

The 4th Space is our delivery layer — the physical, social room that turns a prediction into a person's presence. Where the model says pressure is breaking, the 4th Space is the front door our teams already trust.

Mental healthFrom crisis to company
In Spelthorne, Adur and Glasgow — our highest-anxiety flags — the 4th Space turns a lonely week into a warm hour with people who expect you. No referral, no waiting list, no clinical form. Just warmth, on a schedule. "I am not a case file. I am someone the room expects on Tuesday."
AgeingFrom isolation to ritual
Older people are the quietest pressure in the data and the loneliest in the room. A regular contrast-therapy circle gives the week a shape — a reason to leave the flat, a face that's expecting you. "The circle remembers my name before my GP does."
PovertyFrom scarcity to dignity
When money is thin, public warmth is a luxury. A subsidised 4th Space restores it as a right — sitting beside debt advice and cost-of-living support, but asking for nothing in return. "Warmth isn't a handout. It's something that's mine."
HomelessnessFrom the street to steadiness
Under the 2026 Rough Sleeping Strategy, prevention is the brief. The 4th Space is the steady, repeated contact that keeps someone off the pavement — a place that is theirs, not a waiting room. "First place all week that didn't want me to leave by lunchtime."
NHS strainFrom queue to community
Every avoided lonely evening is a call to the GP that never had to happen. The 4th Space is neighbourhood health delivered by presence, not a prescription. "The appointment I kept was the one with the sauna."
