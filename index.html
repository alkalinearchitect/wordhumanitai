We see community pressure rising before it becomes a crisis. We then connect people to help, in the places that need it most.

HumanitAI is a UK Community Interest Company. We combine predictive intelligence with trusted human connection to help communities act earlier.

Community problems are usually counted too late: homelessness after someone loses their home, hospital demand after health deteriorates, crisis after isolation becomes severe. We bring the analytical system and a physical, welcoming front door. Our pilot will test whether the model works in practice.

01The model

Four connected parts: a human front door, and the intelligence behind it.

HumanitAI has four connected parts. Community-facing activity builds trust and contact; behind it, a responsible early-warning system turns information into guidance. People decide what to do.

1

Community wellbeing: the front door

Sauna, cold immersion and social sessions. Low-stigma spaces where people meet and reconnect. Always voluntary.

2

Predictive intelligence: the engine

Public and community data, combined with AI and forecasting, to detect pressure rising and communities at risk of being overlooked.

3

Decision support: directing help

Clear, practical intelligence on what is changing and where. Charities, councils and funders focus outreach. Humans stay responsible.

4

Community response: action

Intelligence connects to response through local partners, verified directories and navigation. The proposed 5th Space brings it into the places under rising pressure.

02The 5th Space · proposed concept

The front-end is contrast therapy. Sauna, then ice. The connector between a prediction and a person.

A fixed building waits for people to come to it. The 5th Space is our proposed answer: contrast therapy as a low-stigma front door where people meet and reconnect. The intelligence flags a place. The 5th Space is what a person actually meets. Paid sessions also generate sustainable income for the CIC. Proposed concept under development; always voluntary.

04How we build

AI agents, loops and graphs: the engine room.

HumanitAI is built the way modern agent systems are built: small autonomous loops that watch, classify and act, organised into graphs when a single loop would rot.

01

AI agents

Autonomous workers with a trigger, a task, a success bar, re-running until met. Used for intake triage, signal classification, report drafting, with a human in the approval seat.

02

Prompts as programs

The unit of work is the instruction you paste to the agent. Precise, copy-paste-able prompts with acceptance criteria beat vague briefs.

03

Loop engineering

Self-improving loops: heartbeat, cron and webhook triggers; manager triages, worker executes only low-risk tickets; each run pastes evidence before merge.

04

Graph engineering

When one loop rots from context, split into nodes, each its own loop with its own success bar, feeding a synthesizer. Graphs answer "if this fails, where do we roll back".

TRIGGERheartbeat · cron · webhook · new ticket
TASKclassify → build → review → test → open PR with evidence
SUCCESSmeasurable bar met, or fix and re-run from step one
GUARDRAILcap 3 issues/run · read-only backlog · human merge · own worktree

05How we work

Four layers, with governance underneath.

Data layer

Public datasets, community submissions, service directories and outcomes. Anonymous by default. No individual is scored.

Intelligence layer

Statistics and AI clean, classify and detect change. AI recommends. People decide.

Application layer

Website, postcode search, community dashboard, local-help directory, reporting tools.

Governance layer

Privacy, consent, human review, safeguarding, bias testing. Published openly.
