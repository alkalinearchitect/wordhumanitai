02The 5th Space

The 5th Space is a canal barge that sails to the towns the forecast flags — so the help arrives before the crisis does.

The 5th Space · The Forerunner Fleet · off-grid canal barge · at the towns before the crisis

A fixed building waits for people to come to it. The 5th Space goes to them. When the model flags a town tipping into crisis, the barge routes along the canal network and moors there — a warm room, on the water, that lonely people actually return to. It is our answer to the isolation the forecast flags first.

Why a bargeThe 4th Space was a room people had to travel to. The 5th Space travels to the people. Same warmth, no postcode lottery.
The promiseWhen the data says a town is about to tip, we don't send a leaflet. We send a hull, a fridge, and a face that's expecting you.
Arrive beforeYou weren't forgotten. You were forecast — and we moved. The boat doesn't ask if you're worth saving. It just docks.
Mental healthFrom crisis to company
In Spelthorne, Adur and Glasgow — our three highest-anxiety flags — the barge is the one appointment with no referral and no waiting list. A warm hour, every week it's moored, with people who expect you. The forecast decides the mooring; the mooring decides the company.
AgeingFrom isolation to ritual
Older people are the loneliest in the data and the quietest in the room. A regular circle on the water gives the week a shape — a reason to leave the flat, a face that's expecting you, a kettle that's already on.
PovertyFrom scarcity to dignity
When money is thin, warmth is a luxury. A subsidised 5th Space restores it as a right — beside debt advice and cost-of-living support, asking nothing in return, tied to whatever town the model names next.
HomelessnessFrom the street to steadiness
Under the 2026 Rough Sleeping Strategy, prevention is the brief. The 5th Space is the steady contact that keeps someone off the pavement — a place that is theirs, not a waiting room, and it's never more than a canal-branch away.
NHS strainFrom queue to community
Every lonely evening we fill is a call to the GP that never had to happen. Neighbourhood health, delivered by presence — not a prescription, and not a fixed address.
