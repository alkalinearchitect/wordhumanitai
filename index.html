02The 4th Space

The 4th Space: a sauna and cold-plunge venue where lonely people show up — and stop being lonely.

Warm in.
Cold out.
Less alone.
Contrast therapy · connection by design

The 4th Space is where the forecast becomes human — a warm room, on a schedule, that lonely people actually return to. It's our answer to the loneliness the model flags first.

Mental healthFrom crisis to company
In Spelthorne, Adur and Glasgow — our three highest-anxiety flags — the 4th Space is the one appointment with no referral and no waiting list. A warm hour, every week, with people who expect you.
AgeingFrom isolation to ritual
Older people are the loneliest in the data and the quietest in the room. A regular circle gives the week a shape — a reason to leave the flat, a face that's expecting you.
PovertyFrom scarcity to dignity
When money is thin, warmth is a luxury. A subsidised 4th Space restores it as a right — beside debt advice and cost-of-living support, asking nothing in return.
HomelessnessFrom the street to steadiness
Under the 2026 Rough Sleeping Strategy, prevention is the brief. The 4th Space is the steady contact that keeps someone off the pavement — a place that is theirs, not a waiting room.
NHS strainFrom queue to community
Every lonely evening we fill is a call to the GP that never had to happen. Neighbourhood health, delivered by presence — not a prescription.
