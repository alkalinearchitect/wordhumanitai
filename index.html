Predictive community intelligence · with a human front door

We see community pressure rising before it becomes crisis. We then connect people to help, in the places that need it most.

HumanitAI is a UK Community Interest Company. We combine predictive intelligence with trusted human connection to help communities act earlier: bringing together public data, local knowledge and advanced forecasting to identify rising social, health and economic pressure. It directs support, education and prevention towards the people and places where earlier action may have the greatest impact. We are in pilot. We do not yet forecast crises with certainty.

Community problems are usually counted too late: homelessness after someone loses their home, hospital demand after health deteriorates, crisis after isolation becomes severe. We bring together the analytical system and a physical, welcoming front door. Community sauna, cold immersion and social wellbeing. So that insight turns into a relationship, and a relationship turns into early help. Our pilot will test whether the model works in practice.

01The model

Four connected parts: a human front door, and the intelligence behind it.

HumanitAI has four connected parts. The community-facing activity builds trust and contact; behind it, a responsible early-warning system turns information into guidance. People decide what to do.

1

Community wellbeing: the front door

Sauna, cold immersion and social sessions. Low-stigma spaces where people meet and reconnect. Always voluntary.

2

Predictive intelligence: the engine

Public and community data, combined with AI and forecasting, to detect pressure rising together and communities at risk of being overlooked.

3

Decision support: directing help

Clear, practical intelligence on what is changing and where. Charities, councils and funders focus outreach. Humans stay responsible for decisions.

4

Community response: action

Intelligence connects to response through local partners, verified directories and navigation. The proposed 5th Space brings it into the places under rising pressure.

The complete loop: public data and local knowledge reveal emerging pressure → AI and forecasting identify patterns and possible risks → human review turns findings into responsible recommendations → community partners direct support to the right places → physical wellbeing spaces bring people together → outcomes are measured so the system learns.

02The 5th Space · proposed concept

The front-end is contrast therapy. Sauna, then ice. The connector between a prediction and a person.

The 5th Space · proposed concept · sauna + cold plunge · design illustration

A fixed building waits for people to come to it. The 5th Space is our proposed answer: contrast therapy, a sauna then an ice bath, as a low-stigma front door where people meet and reconnect. The intelligence flags a place. The 5th Space is what a person actually meets. Paid sessions also generate sustainable income for the CIC. It is a proposed concept under development, not an operating service, and taking part is always voluntary.

The front doorThe wellbeing service is separate from the analytical system. Sauna, cold immersion and social sessions create accessible, low-stigma spaces that reduce isolation and build trusted community contact.
The connectorA forecast is a dashboard. A person is lonely. The 5th Space is the hinge between them: it turns a prediction into a place, and a place into the early help that stops a crisis.

04The proposed platform

One platform. Several modules. A nervous system for communities.

Not fifteen disconnected websites. A proposed system that observes pressure, connects people to help, exposes institutional friction, and helps test preventive action. This is the platform we are building toward — described here as a proposal, with the prototype dashboard above as the first working piece.

Module 01

Community dashboard

A simple view of current community condition, direction of change, main pressure signals, local support capacity, and data confidence. The prototype above is the first version.

Module 02

Local Help directory

A verified directory of useful support: what it provides, who can access it, opening times, contact, referral requirements, accessibility, and date last verified.

Module 03

Community Signal

A way for residents and organisations to submit observations — a service has closed, waiting times rising, more people need food support, transport is a barrier, a group is becoming isolated.

Module 04

Intervention Lab

A later-stage decision-support tool: compare possible responses (e.g. would £50k help more as debt advice, transport support, community meals or peer groups?). Support, not automatic decisions.

Module 05

Friction Monitor

Measures how hard it is to reach support — waits, forms, repeated referrals, digital-only gates. The route to help is often the real barrier.

Module 06

Impact Tracker

Measures whether conditions improved after action. Every score tagged measured, reported, estimated, predicted or simulated — never false precision.

Data & governance

Any intelligence layer would use public UK data (ONS, MHCLG, NHS England, Companies House, council registers) and community-submitted signals, gathered and scored by free, self-hosted models — no per-query API bills. AI recommends; people decide. AI never decides whether a specific person deserves support.

05How we work

Four layers, with governance underneath.

Data layer

Public datasets, community submissions, service directories and outcomes. Anonymous by default. No individual is scored.

Intelligence layer

Statistics and AI clean, classify and detect change. AI recommends. People decide.

Application layer

Website, postcode search, community dashboard, local-help directory, reporting tools.

Governance layer

Privacy, consent, human review, safeguarding, bias testing. Published openly.
