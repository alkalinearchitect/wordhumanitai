# HumanitAI — Statement of Purpose

HumanitAI is a UK Community Interest Company. Its Community Radar and
Prevention Intelligence Platform helps places **notice deterioration earlier,
coordinate community response, and direct prevention funding using transparent
evidence.**

## Core doctrine
Use intelligence to make community needs visible to institutions, make
institutional response measurable, and direct prevention funding toward the
places and interventions with the strongest evidence of benefit.

## Ethical inversion (what we learn from China, and reject)
China's strongest civic technology closes the loop: a signal enters, is
classified, assigned, acted on, reviewed and converted into system learning.
But China often uses visibility to increase state control over society.

HumanitAI reverses the direction of accountability:
- **Community signals make unmet need visible to institutions.**
- **Public dashboards and intervention records make institutional response
  visible to communities.**

## Non-negotiable design choices
- Aggregate place-level intelligence is the default; individual case data is
  exceptional, consented and separately governed.
- Use official APIs and data-sharing agreements before scraping. Public
  availability is never permission for personal profiling.
- Forecasting estimates what may happen; causal evaluation estimates what
  changes outcomes; MiroFish explores reactions and second-order effects. These
  are different epistemic tasks and must not be conflated.
- Hermes orchestrates but cannot bypass access control, publish externally, or
  alter production data without an approval gate.
- Every recommendation states confidence, assumptions, equity effects,
  alternative explanations and a stop condition.
- Expansion is conditional on independent evidence of benefit — not engagement
  or dashboard activity.

## North-star outcome
More capable and connected residents; lower avoidable crisis demand; accountable
spending decisions.

## The HumanitAI standard
> Intelligence should reduce dependency, reveal root causes and increase the
> community's power to shape the systems that affect it. The platform succeeds
> when fewer people need the platform.
