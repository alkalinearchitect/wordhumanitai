Legal & intellectual property

We didn't build this to own the problem. We built it to end it.

Most legal pages are written so you won't read them. This one is written so you will — because if you understand the terms, you're more likely to use the work. And that's the whole point.

01Why

The insight exists to get ahead of the crisis — not to sit on it.

Every map, model and forecast on this site is built to arrive before the ambulance does. If our reading of the open data helps you act sooner — a council, a clinician, a neighbour — that is the mission doing its job.

So we don't gatekeep the signal. We give it. What follows are the simple rules for doing that honourably.

02How

Share the signal. Credit the source. Don't sell the silence.

You're free to quote, screenshot and redistribute our public forecasts for non-commercial good — as long as HumanitAI is named and the analysis isn't repackaged as your own.

The towns aren't ours. The suffering isn't ours. The insight is ours to give, not to lock behind a paywall.
