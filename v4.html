The model

Three layers working as one.

Intelligence finds where pressure is building. The 5th Space brings people in. Regulation helps them steady themselves — and what we learn feeds back to sharpen the intelligence. People stay at the centre; technology supports, never replaces, human judgement.

LAYER ONE

Artificial Intelligence

The intelligence layer continuously analyses ethically sourced information to understand where pressure is building. It asks "What is likely to happen next?" — prediction, prioritisation, prevention. Not surveillance. Not replacing professionals.

LAYER TWO

The 5th Space

People already spend life in four systems: home, work, education, healthcare. The 5th Space is everything between them — somewhere to enter without diagnosis, appointment, referral, judgement or crisis. Designed for prevention.

LAYER THREE

Evidence-Based Regulation

Stress changes behaviour. When people become chronically dysregulated they are less likely to seek help, connect or decide well. HumanitAI uses interventions that may help people regulate themselves.

The boat

The first 5th Space moves.

Instead of expecting communities to travel to support, support travels to communities. A canal boat becomes a hub, classroom, wellbeing centre and meeting place — reaching towns without permanent infrastructure first.

A floating civic asset, not just transport.

The boat is the most recognisable face of HumanitAI — a mobile 5th Space that brings prevention directly to the people who need it, before a single permanent wall is built.

"We sail before the silence does."

For funders

Grant proposal: £15,000 to deliver a 12-month prevention pilot in Bath.

HumanitAI CIC is a Bristol-based community interest company, piloting first in Bath, Somerset. The pilot turns our open-source pressure-forecast into community ownership: 12 monthly peer workshops, a free open-source toolkit, and 10 trained local volunteers.

Award for All (National Lottery) — rolling decision, around 16 weeks. Every pound is asset-locked to our community mission by law. We report what we measure, not what we hope for.

Scaling

Bath becomes the first pilot. Not because Bath needs HumanitAI most — but because every national system needs somewhere to prove itself.

The goal is replication. The operating model stays the same while each community adapts the physical space to local needs.
