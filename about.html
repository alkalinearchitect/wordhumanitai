HumanitAI CIC

Preventing exclusion before it becomes crisis

Artificial intelligence and digital services are changing how people work, learn, access healthcare and participate in society. But people already experiencing poverty, unemployment, loneliness, disability or low digital confidence face the greatest risk of being left behind. At the same time, public services often recognise problems only after they have become severe.

HumanitAI is developing a community prevention model that combines trusted physical spaces, practical education and responsible predictive intelligence.

01How the model works

Four moves, one prevention loop

We bring people together

Community sauna, supervised cold immersion and accessible wellbeing sessions are proposed to provide a welcoming, low-stigma reason for people to leave isolation, establish positive routines and build trusted relationships.

We build capability

Participants can access practical AI and digital education, employment support, online-safety training, mentoring and guidance into local services and opportunities.

We identify emerging need

HumanitAI combines appropriate public data, anonymous community feedback and knowledge from local organisations. Forecasting, reasoning and scenario-modelling methods help identify where social and economic pressure may be increasing, which communities may be underserved, where services are becoming difficult to access, which pressures are compounding, and where earlier intervention could prevent greater harm. Important findings are reviewed by people. HumanitAI does not diagnose, profile or make automated decisions about individuals.

We connect intelligence with action

The resulting community intelligence helps charities, councils, funders and local organisations understand where outreach, education, wellbeing provision and practical support may be needed most.

We build financial sustainability

Income from paid wellbeing sessions, memberships, organisational bookings and commissioned programmes is intended to subsidise affordable community access, AI and digital education, outreach, support navigation, community research and preventive interventions.

03The proposed pilot

One Bristol community, tested in the open

We are seeking funding to co-design and test the HumanitAI model in one Bristol community. The pilot will examine whether the model can:

Reach people experiencing social or digital exclusion
Increase connection and belonging
Improve AI and digital confidence
Improve access to education, employment and support
Identify emerging community barriers earlier
Produce useful intelligence for local partners
Generate earned income to support long-term sustainability

04Our long-term goal

A replicable prevention model

HumanitAI aims to create a replicable community prevention model that helps people remain connected, capable and supported while helping local organisations act before pressure becomes crisis.

Core proposition HumanitAI combines trusted human connection with predictive community intelligence to direct earlier support where it may create the greatest benefit.
