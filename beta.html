HumanitAI CIC · Why we exist
Not an AI company. Not a wellness company. Not a charity.

We believe pressure should be seen before it becomes a crisis.

Most public services wait until people hit a threshold for help. We exist to change that — because the earlier a community recognises pressure building, the more lives improve and the lower the cost to society. That belief is why HumanitAI exists. The AI, the 5th Space and the boat are simply how we live it.

Why · How · What From Bath to the UK
The Golden Circle

Start with why. Then explain how. Only then, what.

WHY. Communities are left to reach crisis before anyone helps. We believe that is wrong — and preventable. Pressure can be seen early.

HOW. We combine predictive intelligence, a physical 5th Space and evidence-based regulation into one continuous prevention loop. People stay at the centre; technology supports, never replaces, human judgement.

WHAT. A mobile canal boat, AI forecasts on public data, peer workshops, contrast therapy and community meals — the tangible things a town can see and touch.

The Problem

Today, most public services intervene after people reach a threshold for help.

The HumanitAI Model

Three integrated layers. Not a list of tools — a system.

Each layer does nothing alone. Together they form a continuous learning loop where people stay at the centre and technology supports, never replaces, human judgement.

LAYER ONE

Artificial Intelligence

The intelligence layer continuously analyses ethically sourced information to understand where pressure is building. It asks "What is likely to happen next?" — prediction, prioritisation, prevention. Not surveillance. Not replacing professionals.

  • Public datasets
  • Local authority information
  • NHS statistics
  • Community surveys
  • Charity demand
  • Employment indicators
  • Cost-of-living data
  • Population trends
LAYER TWO

The 5th Space

People already spend life in four systems: home, work, education, healthcare. The 5th Space is everything between them — somewhere to enter without diagnosis, appointment, referral, judgement or crisis. Designed for prevention.

  • Conversation
  • Education and movement
  • Sauna and cold immersion
  • Digital support
  • Mentoring and workshops
  • AI education
  • Volunteering
  • Community meals
LAYER THREE

Evidence-Based Regulation

Stress changes behaviour. When people become chronically dysregulated they are less likely to seek help, connect or decide well. HumanitAI uses interventions that may help people regulate themselves.

  • Contrast therapy
  • Movement
  • Breathwork
  • Education
  • Community participation
  • Sleep education
  • Nutrition guidance
AI detects pressure → 5th Space gathers people → regulation engages → evidence improves the model
The Boat

One of the most distinctive ideas: make the first 5th Space mobile.

Instead of expecting communities to travel to support, support travels to communities. A canal boat becomes a hub, classroom, wellbeing centre, AI demonstration space and meeting place — moving through Britain's canals reaching towns and cities.

Community hub and classroom
Wellbeing and contrast-therapy centre
AI demonstration and consultation space
Reaches towns without permanent infrastructure first
A recognisable national symbol of prevention
Every stop creates community, conversation, education, data, evidence, relationships
Why AI Matters

Without AI, everyone gets similar services regardless of local need.

With AI, communities receive support based on emerging pressure. Instead of opening another programme because funding exists, HumanitAI asks: which neighbourhood is beginning to struggle? Which age group is becoming isolated? Which intervention appears most effective? Limited resources have greater impact.

Our capability today: HumanitAI already runs a forecasting prototype on open public datasets — the same engine that would power live community-level pressure signals. In the Bath pilot we take that from prototype to a verified, partner-reviewed service: every forecast checked by a person before it informs any action.

Scaling

Bath becomes the first pilot. Not because Bath needs HumanitAI most — but because every national system needs somewhere to prove itself.

The goal is replication. The operating model stays the same while each community adapts the physical space to local needs.
