The Three-Pillar Prevention Model

Three integrated parts. Not a list of tools — a system.

Each pillar does nothing alone. Together they form a loop: intelligence finds the need, the 5th Space brings people in, contrast therapy keeps them there, and what we learn feeds the intelligence again.

01 / INTELLIGENCE

Artificial Intelligence

The invisible operating system. It reads ethically sourced public and community data to spot where pressure is rising — before it escalates.

  • Where support is needed
  • Which communities are vulnerable
  • Which interventions work best
  • How to allocate resources
02 / PLACE

The 5th Space

A welcoming hub outside home, work, school and clinic. The human interface of HumanitAI — first as a mobile canal boat, later from permanent venues.

  • Build social connection
  • Regulate stress
  • Learn digital and life skills
  • Access help early
03 / ENGAGEMENT

Contrast Therapy

Sauna and cold immersion are the front door. Beyond wellbeing, they create the trust and conversation that connect people to wider support.

  • Social interaction
  • Stress regulation
  • Trust-building
  • Early identification

Why the canal boat

A mobile civic asset, not just transport.

The first 5th Space may be a canal boat. It travels the UK and brings prevention services directly into communities — before a single permanent wall is built.

  • Reach multiple communities without permanent infrastructure first
  • Serve as a recognisable, human HumanitAI hub
  • Bring prevention to people, not wait for them to arrive
  • Demonstrate the model before scaling to fixed locations
  • Gather evidence from different regions while building a national presence
