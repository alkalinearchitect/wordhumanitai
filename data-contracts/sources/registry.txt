# HumanitAI Source Registry

Every data source must be registered before ingestion. Fields follow the
blueprint 5.4 source-registry schema. Live ONS/Nomis/Fingertips/Police.uk
endpoints were verified working from this environment (see
uk-data-scraping-research.md).

- source_id: ons_population_estimates
  owner: data_steward
  url: https://api.beta.ons.gov.uk/v1
  purpose: neighbourhood denominators and trend context
  lawful_basis: public/open statistical data
  contains_personal_data: false
  geography: LSOA/MSOA/local_authority
  refresh: release_triggered
  quality_checks: [schema, null_rate, revision, geography_match]
  retention: versioned_indefinitely
  status: approved

- source_id: nomis_labour_market
  owner: data_steward
  url: https://www.nomisweb.co.uk/api/v01/dataset/NM_1_1.data.csv
  purpose: claimant count, census and small-area indicators
  lawful_basis: public/open statistical data (no auth for bulk CSV)
  contains_personal_data: false
  geography: LSOA/MSOA/local_authority
  refresh: monthly / quarterly
  quality_checks: [schema, null_rate, geography_match]
  retention: versioned_indefinitely
  status: approved

- source_id: fingertips_public_health
  owner: data_steward
  url: https://fingertips.phe.org.uk/api
  purpose: public-health outcomes, risk factors and inequalities
  lawful_basis: public/open statistical data (JSON/CSV)
  contains_personal_data: false
  geography: local_authority / MSOA
  refresh: indicator-dependent
  quality_checks: [schema, revision]
  retention: versioned_indefinitely
  status: approved

- source_id: police_uk
  owner: data_steward
  url: https://data.police.uk/docs/api
  purpose: recorded street crime and neighbourhood data
  lawful_basis: public/open policing data
  contains_personal_data: false
  geography: neighbourhood / LSOA
  refresh: monthly
  quality_checks: [schema, freshness]
  retention: versioned_indefinitely
  status: approved

- source_id: trussell_trust_open
  owner: data_steward
  purpose: emergency food parcel distribution (aggregate)
  lawful_basis: published organisational statistics
  contains_personal_data: false
  geography: UK / region
  refresh: annual
  quality_checks: [provenance, null_rate]
  retention: versioned_indefinitely
  status: approved

- source_id: jrf_uk_poverty
  owner: data_steward
  purpose: UK poverty rates and child poverty by local authority
  lawful_basis: published research statistics
  contains_personal_data: false
  geography: local_authority
  refresh: annual
  quality_checks: [provenance, null_rate]
  retention: versioned_indefinitely
  status: approved
